Meta · AI Glasses

Putting maps on a new screen, and making people want it there

In leading end-to-end GTM for the first maps product on Meta's AI glasses, I shaped the launch strategy for navigation as a beta feature (unprecedented for Meta's AI glasses), defined positioning, messaging, and naming, and influenced channel strategy to elevate it as a hero feature across marketing and sales.

The result: Navigation featured at Meta Connect and a subway station takeover in San Francisco's Powell St., to become a top 3 purchase intent driver for AI glasses.

At Skydio, I partnered closely with the Sales team to understand customer needs. As a direct answer to their feedback, I built visual, segment-specific battle cards that translated technical imaging specs into a clear, compelling illustration of value for customers.
